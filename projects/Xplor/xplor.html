Chapter 01

Understanding Xplor AI Mobility

Founded in late 2023 at the Kerala Technology Innovation Zone in Kochi, Xplor AI Mobility is an Intelligent Mobility as a Service (iMaaS) company building a connected digital ecosystem for public transportation and fleet management.

The Real-World Crisis

In India, millions rely on private buses and public transit daily. However, operations are plagued by uncoordinated schedules, manual paper tickets, zero live arrival tracking, and complete disconnect between metro and feeder services.

AI-Powered iMaaS

Xplor transforms fragmented transit by connecting operators, vehicles, drivers, and commuters through a centralized software platform—integrating ticketing, automated scheduling, GPS telemetry, and passenger discovery.

Multimodal Vision

From Kochi Metro feeder bus tie-ups to Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC) mobility protocols, Xplor unites isolated transit modes into a single, seamless commuter journey.

Xplor User App

A commuter-facing application enabling users to search routes, check real-time bus arrivals, buy metro/bus digital tickets, and plan multimodal journeys across the city.

Fleet Operations Platform

Tools for bus owners and transit authorities to monitor fleets in real-time, audit ticketing revenue, manage crew schedules, and improve route efficiency.

Kochi Metro Feeder Network

Digital scheduling and first/last-mile integration connecting Kochi Metro stations with feeder buses, reducing commuter transfer wait times.

Halo by Xplor

A dedicated school and institutional transportation ERP providing parent ETA alerts, route optimization, driver monitoring, and child safety attendance.

Project 01 Strategic Product Research & Heuristic Benchmarking NDA Protected

Global Transit & Mobility Platform UX Benchmarking

Scope: 42 International & Domestic Transit Applications
Duration: 6-Day Competitive Sprint
Deliverable: Heuristic Evaluation Matrix & 9 Systemic Opportunity Areas
Key Focus: Multimodal Discovery, Live Telemetry & Demand Capture

To transition Xplor from an isolated bus-ticketing tool into an intelligent, end-to-end urban mobility companion, I conducted an exhaustive benchmarking study of 42 global and domestic transit applications. The study categorized platforms across three primary clusters:

  • Global Multimodal Leaders: Citymapper, Moovit, Transit App, Whim, Chalo, Tummoc (evaluated for multimodal routing, walking radii, transfer clarity, and disruption handling).
  • Ride-Hailing & Intercity Mobility: Uber, Rapido, Namma Yatri, RedBus, ixigo, Abhibus, MakeMyTrip, Grab, Waze (evaluated for pin accuracy, fare breakdown, and post-booking reassurance).
  • Open Mobility & Regional Networks: ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) mobility protocol, Kochi One, BMTC Namma, Dilli Sarthi (evaluated for multi-operator ticketing and unified digital passes).
Opportunity 01

Multimodal Journey Planning

Transitioning from single-mode bus routes to combined bus + metro + walking transfers with dynamic time-to-leave indicators.

Opportunity 02

Live GPS Vehicle Telemetry

Displaying vehicle registration numbers, live speed status, and reliable arrival countdowns rather than static static timetable estimates.

Opportunity 03

First & Last-Mile Feeders

Explicitly linking metro station gates to feeder bus stops and auto-rickshaw stands to solve the last-mile transfer friction.

Opportunity 04

Demand Capture on Unserved Routes

Converting dead-end search queries into a crowdsourced voting mechanism that signals high-density unserved commuter corridors to bus operators.

Opportunity 05

Digital Transit Passes

Introducing weekly/monthly student and commuter passes with offline QR validation, bypassing individual ticket queues.

Opportunity 06

Low-Connectivity Resilience

Caching active digital tickets and recently searched route maps locally on the client to guarantee inspection validity in underground or remote dead zones.

Strategic Impact

Synthesized into a 28-slide executive benchmark presentation adopted by product leadership as the UX roadmap for Xplor's multimodal expansion.

Project 02 Commuter Experience & Interaction Architecture NDA Protected

Xplor Commuter App: Core UX Enhancements & Journey Logic

Duration: 02 June – 20 July 2026 (49-Day Sprint)
Methodology: Usability Testing Analysis, Heuristic Evaluation, Wireframing
Core Modules: Search Modification, 3-Scenario Availability, Bus Card & Schedule Redesign

Through moderated user-testing sessions and bug reports, we identified critical usability bottlenecks in the core commuter flow: users struggled to understand whether their route modifications had taken effect, encountered unhelpful dead ends when direct buses were unavailable, and faced high cognitive strain when scanning bus cards in moving vehicles.

I redesigned the core journey planning architecture across three foundational interaction flows:

Flow 2.1: Search & Location Modification State Logic

The Friction: In the legacy build, selecting boarding and destination stops automatically advanced to results. While efficient initially, when commuters tapped back to swap or modify stops, the app silently auto-advanced again without an explicit confirmation state, causing disorientation.

01. Home Search
02. Select Boarding & Destination
03. Auto-Proceed to Results
04. Return to Modify / Swap Stops
05. Explicit "Proceed" CTA Trigger
06. Confirmed Updated Results

✓ Result: Zero accidental route queries; standardized seamlessly across both Bus and Metro search engines.

Flow 2.2: The 3-Scenario Bus Availability Architecture
Scenario 1

Direct Bus Available

Immediate display of partner buses operating directly between Stop A and Stop B, sorted by live arrival countdown and seat availability.

Outcome: Fast 1-tap checkout & live GPS track
Scenario 2

No Direct Bus (Alternative Options)

Legacy bug previously showed irrelevant cross-town routes. Redesigned to surface genuine nearby alternative boarding stops and viable transfer connections.

Outcome: Actionable alternate travel options
Scenario 3

No Service Available (Dead-End Redesign)

Replaced dead-end "No services found" with an interactive "Request This Route" engine. Commuters input travel frequency, report operating private buses, and receive queue position updates.

Outcome: Crowdsourced fleet expansion intelligence

Flow 2.3: Bus Card & Schedule Information Architecture Overhaul

User testing in transit showed commuters struggled to compare buses while standing in moving vehicles. The legacy card mixed registration numbers, fares, and timings with flat optical weight. I re-architected the information hierarchy into distinct cognitive tiers:

Tier 1 · Primary

Bus Reg. Number & Fleet Operator

High-contrast badge for instant physical curb recognition.

Tier 2 · Decision Time

Live Arrival Countdown & Fare

Dynamic ETA (e.g., "Arriving in 4 min") paired with exact ticket fare.

Tier 3 · Utilities

GPS Status & E-Ticket Pass

Live GPS signal indicator and direct tap for full route stop sequence.

User Testing Validation

Moderated prototype testing showed a 38% reduction in search modification errors and an 85% task completion rate on bus schedule evaluation.

Project 03 Multimodal Transit Systems & ONDC Integration NDA Protected

Kochi Metro Competitor Study & Multimodal Booking Architecture

Scope: 30 Metro & Transit Platforms across India + ONDC Open Mobility
Duration: 16 June – 23 July 2026 (8-Day Research & Flow Architecture)
Deliverable: 14-Page Metro Research Document & 4-Pillar Feature Architecture

While Xplor successfully operated single-ticket QR booking for the Kochi Metro, the company needed a strategic blueprint to expand into larger, multi-line transit networks across India. Additionally, the Indian Government's ONDC (Open Network for Digital Commerce) open mobility protocols were dismantling isolated ticketing silos.

I benchmarked 30 metro applications across Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, and Kochi. The research revealed that leading platforms support commuters across multiple dimensions beyond ticket purchasing. I translated these insights into an integrated 4-Pillar Multimodal Architecture:

Pillar 1: Metro Schedules & Station Intelligence

First & last train departure schedules, peak/off-peak frequency intervals, line interchange guidance, platform direction indicators, and accessible elevator gate locators.

Pillar 2: Kochi Metro Feeder Bus Connectivity

Direct scheduling synchronization between incoming metro trains and station feeder shuttle buses, allowing commuters to plan their last-mile connection before exiting the fare gates.

Pillar 3: Station Parking Availability

Live occupancy indicators for two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking lots across metro stations, eliminating commuter anxiety when driving to park-and-ride hubs.

Pillar 4: "Near Me" Destination Discovery

Geofenced exploration connecting metro exits to key hospitals, colleges, IT parks, and cultural attractions, establishing the app as a city discovery companion.

Multimodal Information Architecture

Designed the pre-booking, active-transit, and post-booking states to seamlessly connect metro ticket generation with feeder bus timetable tracking.

Project 04 Quality Assurance & Usability Engineering NDA Protected

Cross-Platform Usability & Bug Regression Audit (Android & iOS)

Testing Scope: Android (Staging API APK) & iOS (TestFlight Builds)
Duration: 07 July – 13 July 2026 (7-Day Sprint)
Documentation: 6-Page Android Matrix · 7-Page iOS Matrix
Deliverable: Cross-Functional Handoff & Engineering Triage Walkthrough

A flawless UI design is only as good as its production implementation. To ensure design fidelity and robust interaction performance, I conducted a rigorous, three-way regression audit comparing the approved Figma design systems against updated Android builds (accessed via Xplor staging APIs) and iOS builds (via TestFlight).

The audit went beyond visual styling discrepancies to document critical interaction failures, edge cases on low-spec devices, and back-stack navigation bugs.

Platform Severity Audited Core Flow Observed Usability Friction UX & Engineering Resolution
Android (6 Pages) P1 Severe Bus Search & Location Inputs Virtual keyboard occluded the destination suggestion list on sub-6" budget devices. Implemented dynamic viewport scroll repositioning and condensed top app bar padding.
Android (6 Pages) P1 Severe Live Map Vehicle Tracking GPS vehicle marker snapped erratically when commuter cellular connection throttled to 2G. Introduced dead-reckoning interpolation and cached polyline smoothing between pings.
iOS (7 Pages) P2 Polish Navigation Stack Interactive edge-swipe to dismiss results screen wiped selected filter parameters. Persisted active filter state in temporary session cache to allow seamless re-entry.
iOS (7 Pages) P0 Blocker SOS Emergency Trigger Emergency call action sheet was dismissed without confirmation on background tap. Made emergency modal persistent with explicit modal swipe-lock and 5-second countdown.
Both OS P2 Polish Design System Alignment Discrepancies in button radii, elevation shadows, and font weights across OS native toolkits. Standardized design token mapping across Material 3 and Cupertino native UI libraries.
Cross-Team Handoff

Conducted structured triage walkthroughs with mobile engineering leads, prioritizing fixes across release sprints based on commuter friction impact.

Project 05 User Lifecycle Architecture & Contextual Engagement NDA Protected

In-App Push Notification Architecture & Commuter Lifecycle Taxonomy

Duration: 20 July – 21 July 2026 (2-Day System)
Component Specs: Collapsed: 360 × 80 px · Expanded: 360 × 160 px
Taxonomy: 5 Contextual Commuter Lifecycle Segments

Push notifications in a transit companion application must be treated with surgical precision: an irrelevant notification is annoying, but an unread arrival alert results in a missed bus or late transfer. I developed a unified notification system covering both visual component hierarchy across iOS & Android and a context-aware content strategy.

Standardized Notification Component Specs
Collapsed State · 360 × 80 px

Optimized for lock-screen glanceability: App Title (14px) · Notification Title (17px Bold) · Body Message Preview (15px) · Relative Timestamp (13px).

Expanded State · 360 × 160 px

Actionable deep-links revealed upon interaction: Live ETA countdown progress bar, quick-tap "View Live Map", and 1-tap digital ticket QR display.

Project 06 Enterprise SaaS & Public Safety Architecture NDA Protected

"Halo by Xplor": Institutional & School Transport ERP Benchmarking

Platform: Halo by Xplor (School & Campus Transportation ERP)
Duration: 16 July – 21 July 2026 (6-Day Sprint)
Scope: 7 Global & Domestic Transport ERP Platforms Benchmarked
Competitors: MoveInSync, Entab, Fedena, TripSpark, BusBoss, BusRight

Beyond public city buses, Xplor developed Halo, a dedicated enterprise software suite managing school and institutional transportation. Managing student transit introduces intense safety responsibilities: parents require absolute certainty regarding their child's location, while school transport managers must supervise complex multi-vehicle fleets, driver background compliances, and fuel expenses.

I executed a competitor benchmarking analysis across 7 enterprise school transit platforms, extracting best practices and designing the product blueprint across 4 user touchpoints:

Parent Touchpoint

Guardian Reassurance & Live ETA Tracking

Real-time GPS bus tracking with geofenced arrival alerts sent 5 minutes before pickup, eliminating morning roadside waits. Direct integration with WhatsApp emergency broadcasts for sudden schedule changes or bad-weather closures.

Driver / Attendant Touchpoint

High-Contrast In-Vehicle Checklist UI

Large-touch-target tablet interface enabling attendants to perform rapid RFID student check-ins/check-outs, view turn-by-turn navigation with restricted speed zones, and trigger a 1-tap SOS alarm.

School Administration Touchpoint

Fleet Dispatch & Route Optimization

Intelligent routing algorithms that consolidate pickup stops, reduce fuel expenditures, track driver speeding compliance, and generate automated government transport regulatory audits.

Safety & Compliance Touchpoint

Automated Incident Protocols

Instant automated alerts triggered if a bus deviates from its approved geofenced corridor, exceeds regional school-zone speed limits, or remains stationary for an abnormal period with students on board.
